An original, complete open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked metropolis of Coral Bay. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel of high-performance vehicles, navigate ambient traffic and living pedestrians, build your criminal empire across multi-stage story missions, survive intense police pursuits with a dynamic wanted system, and engage in tactical combat.

## Features

- **Living Open World**: A detailed sun-soaked modern coastal city with distinct districts (Ocean Promenade, Downtown Spire, Neon Palms, Industrial Docks, and Marina Cay), complete with asphalt road networks, sidewalks, palm trees, streetlights, and ocean waves.
- **On-Foot & Vehicle Gameplay**: Seamlessly switch between exploring on foot (walk, sprint, melee combat, firearms) and high-speed driving (realistic vehicle physics, acceleration, handbrake drifting, vehicle damage, and carjacking).
- **Vehicles to Steal & Drive**:
  - *Venom GT*: Exotic ultra-performance supercar with blazing acceleration
  - *Vindicator*: Heavy American muscle car with raw torque and drift control
  - *Stallion LX*: Luxury executive sport sedan
  - *CBPD Interceptor*: High-speed pursuit cruiser with working sirens and push bar
- **Intelligent Traffic & Pedestrians**: Ambient civilian vehicles following lane discipline and yielding at junctions; animated pedestrians walking sidewalks and reacting dynamically to gunfire or reckless driving.
- **Wanted Level & Police Response**: Dynamic 1-5 star escalation. Police patrol cruisers respond to crimes, initiate roadblocks, coordinate pit maneuvers, and deploy officers on foot.
- **Story Missions & Progression**:
  - *Mission 1: Neon Arrival* — Meet contact Rico Cruz at Palm Point and shake rival scouts.
  - *Mission 2: High-Octane Repo* — Infiltrate Downtown Spire, steal the Venom GT, and escape heat.
  - *Mission 3: Warehouse Raid* — Strike the syndicate stash at the Docks, neutralize guards, and secure cash.
  - *Mission 4: Marina Grand Heist* — Breach the Marina vault, survive a 4-star citywide CBPD dragnet, and deliver the score to the safehouse.
- **Arsenal & Combat**: Fists, Hawk .45 Pistol, Viper 9mm SMG, and Titan Assault Rifle with projectile physics, muzzle flash, impact sparks, and weapon switching.
- **Full HUD & GPS Minimap**: Real-time rotating minimap with street grid, player blip, police radius, mission waypoints, speedometer, health/armor gauges, wanted stars, and dialogue feed.
- **Audio & Radio Stations**: Procedural Web Audio engine synthesizing engine acoustics, tire screeches, sirens, weapon blasts, and 3 procedural radio stations (Coral Bay Wave, Sunspire FM, Sub City Radio).
- **Save System & Start Screen**: Persistent progression, completed missions, high scores, and cash balance saved locally.
- **100% Offline & Zero Dependencies**: Runs directly from static files in `game/index.html` with zero external network requests.

## How to Play

Start the local server:
```bash
node serve.ts
```
Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in any modern desktop browser (Chrome recommended).

### Controls

- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move character / Steer vehicle
- **Shift**: Sprint on foot / Nitro boost in car
- **E / F / Enter**: Enter or exit vehicle (carjack)
- **Left Mouse Click / Space**: Attack / Shoot / Punch
- **Right Mouse Click**: Aim weapon
- **1 - 4 Keys**: Select weapon (Fists, Pistol, SMG, Rifle)
- **R**: Reload / Change radio station
- **Tab / Esc**: Pause menu & map toggle
